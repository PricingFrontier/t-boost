  TBOOST_EXP_GRAD_SLACK          overrides the multiclass graduation 1% brake (to get ungated candidates)

Units: test/train deviance = weighted mean multiclass cross-entropy (NLL); changes in % of the reference.
"first", "joint2", "diag2" are the second-order decomposition of a move a -> b in NLL units:
  first = mean (p - y).delta, joint2 = 1/2 mean [sum p delta^2 - (sum p delta)^2], diag2 = 1/2 mean sum p(1-p) delta^2.
Cells = deployed_census dense cells (factored tables are not counted, so unbanded banks undercount).
